STARDRIFT Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

STARDRIFT is a space arcade game published by Nwa-chi. This policy explains what information the game uses and how it is handled.

Information used by the game

  • Players do not need an account to play STARDRIFT.
  • High scores are stored locally on the player's device and are not uploaded by the game.
  • The game does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • The game retrieves public configuration and leaderboard information from Google Firebase over an encrypted connection.

Owner administration

A separate owner-only web administration area uses Google Firebase Authentication. When the owner signs in, Firebase processes the owner's email address and authentication credentials. This area is not required to play the game and is not offered as a player account system.

Third-party services

STARDRIFT uses Google Firebase for secure owner authentication, public game configuration, leaderboard storage, and website hosting. Google may process technical information needed to provide and protect those services under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

STARDRIFT is not designed to request personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided through the service, contact us so it can be reviewed.

Security and retention

Network connections use HTTPS. Local high scores remain on the device until the player clears app data or removes the app. Owner authentication data is managed through Firebase.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
