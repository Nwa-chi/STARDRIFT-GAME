## Short description

## Full description

Take the controls and drift into a fast-moving field of stars and asteroids.

STARDRIFT is a focused space arcade challenge built around quick reactions and smooth controls. Guide your ship through an endless hazard field, collect radiant stars to increase your score, and survive as the pace becomes more demanding.

Features:

- Fast, responsive touch controls
- Keyboard controls for desktop play
- Endless score-chasing gameplay
- Increasing difficulty
- Local high-score tracking
- Pause and resume at any time
- No advertising
- No player account required

Every run is another chance to travel farther and set a new personal record.

part of the consumer Play experience. Reviewer credentials are therefore not required.

## Data Safety worksheet

Observed consumer behavior in the current source:

- No player account is required.
- Player high scores are stored locally and are not uploaded.
- No advertising or analytics SDK is included.
- Public configuration and leaderboard data are downloaded from Firebase over HTTPS.
- A separate owner-only web administration route uses Firebase Authentication.

The Play Console account owner must review and confirm every declaration before submission, especially
if monetization, analytics, player accounts, cloud score submission, or additional SDKs are added.
